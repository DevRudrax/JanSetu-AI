## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-20

### 🚀 Initial Production Release
- **Welfare Schemes Discovery Engine**: Semantic citizen profile matching across Central & State schemes with confidence scores and multi-step Application Wizard.
- **Automated RTI Assistant**: Legal draft generator pursuant to Section 6(1) of the RTI Act, 2005 with instant court-admissible PDF export.
- **Smart Grievance Redressal**: AI complaint tone restructuring and Web3Forms email dispatcher with tracking numbers.
- **Sovereign Document Vault**: DigiLocker syncing for Aadhaar, PAN, Land Records, and AI legal notification simplifier.
- **Multi-Lingual Voice Support**: Localization across 12+ Indian languages with Bhashini-compatible Web Speech recognition and audio synthesis.
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Standalone manifest, offline resilience, and network latency health monitor.
- **Accessibility & WCAG AAA**: High contrast mode, dyslexia typography scaling, and global keyboard hotkeys (`Alt+1` to `Alt+5`, `?`).
- **Sovereign Cloud & DB**: Supabase PostgreSQL schema with RLS security policies and fallback offline storage.
