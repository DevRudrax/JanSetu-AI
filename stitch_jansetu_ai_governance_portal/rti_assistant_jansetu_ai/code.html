live_help Common Queries

What is the standard RTI fee? expand_more

The standard application fee is ₹10. BPL (Below Poverty Line) cardholders are completely exempt from this fee. Additional nominal costs may apply if you require physical photocopies of documents.

What if I don't get a reply in 30 days? expand_more

If the 30-day limit passes without a response, it is deemed a refusal. You hold the right to file a First Appeal with the designated Appellate Authority within the same department.

Can I ask 'Why' questions? expand_more

The RTI Act applies to material records existing in files. Asking 'why' a decision was made is often rejected unless the reasons are explicitly recorded in official file notings or minutes.
