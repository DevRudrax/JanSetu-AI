GOVERNMENT OF INDIA

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

Date: 15th February 2024

Official Notification

Subject: Mandatory e-KYC for PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi Beneficiaries.

In reference to the ongoing implementation of the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme, it is hereby notified that all registered beneficiaries must complete their e-KYC verification process to ensure the uninterrupted release of subsequent installment payments. The e-KYC can be completed through OTP-based authentication on the PM-KISAN portal or via biometric authentication at the nearest Common Service Centres (CSC).

Furthermore, beneficiaries are advised to ensure their bank accounts are successfully seeded with their Aadhaar numbers and that Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) is enabled for the respective accounts. Failure to comply with these procedural requirements by the stipulated deadline of 31st March 2024 will result in the temporary suspension of fund transfers. State nodal officers are directed to facilitate widespread awareness campaigns regarding this mandate.

Joint Secretary to the Govt. of India
